# Outdoor Roadway & Street Lighting Engineering Design Skill

## Overview
This skill executes comprehensive engineering design for outdoor roadway lighting, street lighting, perimeter security lighting, and parking areas in full compliance with **CIE 115:2010**, **EN 13201**, and **IESNA RP-8**.

## Key Technical Formulations
1. **Road Luminance & Illuminance**:
   - Road surface classes (R1, R2, R3, R4) and $q_0$ luminance coefficient.
   - Road lighting criteria: Average luminance $L_{avg}$ ($\text{cd/m}^2$), overall uniformity $U_0 = L_{min} / L_{avg}$, longitudinal uniformity $U_l = L_{min} / L_{max}$, and glare control (Threshold Increment $TI \le 10-15\%$).
2. **Pole Spacing & Geometry**:
   - Mounting height $H$ ($6\text{ m}$ to $14\text{ m}$).
   - Spacing-to-height ratio $S/H \approx 3.0 \text{ to } 4.0$.
   - Arrangement configurations: Single-sided, staggered, opposite, and median double-arm twin poles.
3. **Electrical Distribution & Voltage Drop**:
   - Balanced 3-phase R-S-T cyclic connection along lighting poles to minimize neutral current.
   - Voltage drop over extended cable runs ($L > 500\text{ m}$) constrained to $\le 5.0\%$.
   - Individual pole fuse cut-out boxes (6A/10A gG) and earthing rods per pole.
