## 2. Photometric & Luminance Analysis (CIE 115 / EN 13201)

The average illuminance ($E_{avg}$) on the road surface is calculated using the lumen method:

$$E_{avg} = \frac{N_{lum} \times \Phi_{lum} \times UF \times MF}{L \times W}$$

Where:
- $\Phi_{lum} = {{ photometrics.luminaire_lumens }}\text{ lm}$ (LED Power: {{ photometrics.luminaire_power_w }} W)
- $UF = {{ photometrics.utilization_factor }}$ (Utilization Factor)
- $MF = {{ photometrics.maintenance_factor }}$ (Maintenance Factor)
- Calculated Average Illuminance: **{{ photometrics.e_avg_lux }} Lux**

Road surface average luminance ($L_{avg}$) for standard asphalt (CIE Class R3, $q_0 = 0.07$):

$$L_{avg} \approx q_0 \times E_{avg} = 0.07 \times {{ photometrics.e_avg_lux }} = {{ photometrics.l_avg_cd_m2 }} \text{ cd/m}^2$$
