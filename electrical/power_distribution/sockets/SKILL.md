# Small Power & Socket Circuit Design Skill

## 1. Overview & Purpose
This skill deterministically designs small power distribution circuits:
1. Groups general convenience sockets into branch circuits respecting code thresholds (max 10 outlets for SBC/IEC, 8 for NEC).
2. Allocates dedicated high-power branch circuits for heavy appliances (Water Heaters, AC split units, Kitchen equipment).
3. Automatically triggers the **Cross-Discipline Coordinator** to ensure AC thermostats, isolators, and water heater DP switches are not omitted.
4. Sizes circuit breakers ($I_n$) and cable cross-sections ($I_z$).
5. Checks voltage drop ($V_d \le 3\%$).
6. Multi-deliverables generation (Calculation Note, Circuit Schedule Excel, and AutoCAD Homerun tags).

---

## 2. When to Use This Skill
- Designing electrical sockets, convenience receptacles, and kitchen/water heater/AC feeds for residential, commercial, or institutional projects.
- Input requests such as: *"Design small power circuits for 3 bedrooms (18 sockets total), a kitchen (8 sockets + refrigerator + microwave + dishwasher), and 2 water heaters under SBC 401"*.
